# Replenishment and ordering — 2026-10-07

## Local implementation

POS ordering has a persistent paginated selling catalog, name/SKU search,
warehouse availability/backorder labels, independent local cart notifications,
quantity editing and a reachable review action. Cart edits perform no catalog
reads. Scoped memory/disk caches retain visible rows while refreshing; identical
reads share work and use the existing three-read budget. Stale entry refreshes
only the first catalog page rather than replaying all previously loaded pages.
Search examines bounded 25-product pages, automatically advancing empty result
pages; large catalogs can still require several network round trips. No search
index backfill or additional dependency was introduced.

Branch defaults and remembered delivery preferences prefill ordering. Drafts
win over defaults, selected product quotes are checked in one bounded read, and
changed prices require approval. An uncertain submission preserves its immutable
operation ID and payload and pauses cart editing until replay is acknowledged.
Serialized draft writes flush before dismissal; accepted carts clear only after
acknowledgement, without a late write recreating them. Shared company forms use
that same serialized writer to protect request/mapping drafts.

Low-stock queries filter the local durable projection before pagination, including
products beyond the old 50-result window. Threshold is 10 retail base units,
target 20, with ceiling conversion to company units. Owner-confirmed mappings
and confirmed receipt mappings are reused; unmapped products require explicit
matching. Mapped suggestions support selected additions without replacing
existing cart quantities. Suggestions use device/branch inventory and do not
subtract incoming purchases or predict sales.

Sourcing requests are separate from purchases, visible even with an empty
catalog. Store Owner can submit/read own branch requests. Company Owner and
assigned Warehouse Operations can respond, optionally link a selling product,
and close a request; linked products require owner ordering confirmation.
Requests create no stock, reservation, payment or purchase facts. Current role,
warehouse/branch scope, revisions and replay receipts are checked server-side.
No rider or company access to private retail mappings/preferences is granted.
