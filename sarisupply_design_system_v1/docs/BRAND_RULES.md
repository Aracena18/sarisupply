# SariSupply Brand Rules

## Personality
Reliable. Modern. Warm. Premium. Local. Operational.

## Color proportion
Typical operational screen:
- 80–88% warm neutral / white
- 8–15% brand orange
- 2–5% semantic colors

Orange is energy, not wallpaper.

## Logo-derived graphic language

### Supply Slats
Descending modular forms derived from the logo.
Use at 3–6% opacity in large empty areas and onboarding surfaces.

### The Cut
A clipped/diagonal corner may be used on featured brand cards.
Do not apply it to every card.

### Supply Line
Use the single orange route line for progress, replenishment, delivery, and flow concepts.

### Shelf Grid
Use subtle separators and aligned modules to structure information instead of floating cards everywhere.

## Photography / illustration
Operational screens should not rely on decorative imagery.
Use product photography only where it supports product recognition.
Illustrations should be rare and built from the brand geometry.
